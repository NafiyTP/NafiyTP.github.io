Research projects

  • Induction circuits in transformers vs. state-space models. Personal project, 2026.
    Trained a small transformer and a minimal Mamba model (pure PyTorch) on an in-context induction task, then localized the circuit with activation patching. Both reach similar accuracy (0.854 vs. 0.869). In the transformer the circuit sits in two heads of layer 1; in Mamba it is spread over the whole layer, with no single channel necessary or sufficient. The hypothesis "slow pole = important channel" did not hold (near-zero correlation).
    PyTorch · TransformerLens · interpretability

  • RootCause-SLM: log anomaly detection with a small explainer model. Personal project, May-Jul 2026.
    Anomaly detection on HDFS logs with logistic regression on per-block template counts (chronological split), also tested on BGL. A Qwen2.5-1.5B model fine-tuned with LoRA (SFT) on teacher annotations explains the flagged blocks; it finds the anomaly in about 63% of cases, versus 67% for the teacher.
    LoRA · SFT · distillation · ROUGE-L / BERTScore

  • SportsAnalytics: pose estimation and time series foundation models. Team project, ongoing.
    Pipeline inspired by Pose2Sim and OpenCap: 2D keypoint extraction with RTMPose, then a time series foundation model for movement analysis and performance assessment.
    pose estimation · time series

Engineering and course projects

  • Secretary-AI. Team project, Mar-Jun 2025.
    Multi-agent system that automates tasks of a medical secretary: appointments, email, prescriptions, and voice calls to reschedule. A common interface switches between an external API and local inference with Hugging Face Transformers on GPU.

  • Monte Carlo option pricing engine in C++.
    Simulation of Black-Scholes paths from scratch, with variance reduction and a study of convergence error.

  • Numerical model of the global carbon cycle. Course project, Télécom Paris.
    Euler, Heun, RK4 and Adams-Moulton 3 integrators, with convergence, consistency and stability analysis, and climate projections.
